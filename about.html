About us

One kettle. One oven.
One slightly unreasonable dream.

We opened Bagel Chef because finding a proper bagel in Singapore took more effort than it deserved. Now we make them ourselves — the slow, honest, kettle-boiled way.

The whole team 👋
Since 2023

It started with a homesick New Yorker

Our founder spent four years in Brooklyn, came home to Singapore, and couldn't stop complaining about steamed bagels masquerading as the real thing. So in 2023, she rented a tiny shopfront in Tiong Bahru, bought a kettle bigger than her sense of caution, and started boiling.

Today we bake in small batches from 5 AM, sell out embarrassingly early on weekends, and know half our regulars by their order. The other half know the dogs. It's that kind of shop.

We're stubborn about three things: real fermentation, a proper kettle, and never — ever — slicing a bagel all the way through.

The process

Boil. Bake. Serve.

Three steps. No shortcuts. This is the difference between a bagel and round bread.

Boil 🔥

Every bagel takes a swim in kettle water before it ever sees an oven. That's what builds the glossy, crackly crust and the dense, chewy inside. Steamed bagels? Not in this house.

Bake 🥯

Straight from kettle to deck oven, in small batches through the day — 6:30 AM, 8, 10:30, 1 PM, 4 PM. If you time it right, you'll catch them still steaming.

Serve 😋

Sliced (never all the way through), schmeared thick, and handed over with both hands. Cream cheese whipped fresh every morning. No apologising for generous portions.
