Our story

Serious about bagels. Not serious about much else.

Bagel Chef started with one oven, one kettle, and one stubborn belief: Singapore deserves a proper bagel. The kind with a crackly crust and a chew that fights back a little.

Every morning, our dough is hand-rolled, boiled in a kettle (never steamed — we can tell, and so can you), and baked fresh before the sun properly wakes up. Life's short. Get the everything bagel.
